Questions people ask first

When should we start?

As early as you are both willing. Couples who start before the engagement have more room to act on what they learn.

Does this replace our church's premarital class?

It can stand alone or go alongside it. Tell us what your pastor requires and we will work with it.

Can we do it if we live in different cities?

Yes. Sessions are by phone or video, so each of you can join from where you are.
