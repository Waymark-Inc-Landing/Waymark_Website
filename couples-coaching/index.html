Questions people ask first

Is couples coaching the same as marriage counseling?

No. Coaching is structured, practical work on your relationship: a plan you follow session by session. We are not licensed counselors or therapists and we do not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.

Can one of us start alone?

Yes. Sometimes only one person is ready to begin. That still helps, and it is a good place to start.

Do we have to live in Oklahoma?

No. Most sessions happen by phone or video, so we work with couples anywhere in the United States. In-person sessions are available in Shawnee, OK.
