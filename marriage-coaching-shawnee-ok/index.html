Before you begin

Questions people ask first

Where do sessions take place in Shawnee?

In person in Shawnee, by appointment. When you book, we confirm the time and the place with you. Phone and video sessions are also available.

Do you work with couples from the towns around Shawnee?

Yes. Tecumseh, McLoud, Seminole, Prague and the rest of Pottawatomie County are a short drive from us, and phone or video sessions are available too.

Is this marriage counseling?

No. It is coaching: structured, practical work on your marriage. We are not licensed counselors or therapists, and if licensed care is what you need we will say so and help you find it.
