What is a waymark?

A firm sign that says — the path is still here.

A waymark is a marker placed along a trail — a stone, a post, a painted blaze — left by those who walked before so that no one who follows loses the way. When the road is overgrown and the fog rolls in, a waymark is what tells a weary traveler they are not lost. They are simply between one marker and the next.

That is what we do. We mark the way back — to your marriage, to your family, to your faith, to yourself.

We know what it's like to lose our way, and we know what it's like to find the way back. God restored our own marriage, and that story is why we do what we do today.

Waymark is the new chapter of Relationship Resources, a ministry with more than five decades of history. We are honored to carry forward the legacy and methodology of Dr. Jim Talley, walking alongside marriages and families through coaching, workshops, conferences, and publications, in both Spanish and English.

We believe with all our hearts that there is always hope for a marriage. No matter how far the road seems, the way back is possible.

Married for 15 years, we are parents of six and grandparents, and we call Oklahoma home. Our passion is to walk beside you as we mark the way back.

Before you begin

Questions people ask first

Is this counseling or coaching?

Coaching. We are not therapists, and we do not provide counseling, therapy, or treatment. Waymark offers faith-based coaching and spiritual direction, built on Dr. Jim Talley’s decades of relationship work. If licensed care is what you need, we will gladly point you toward it.

Do you work with couples outside Oklahoma?

Yes. Most sessions happen by phone or video, so we walk with couples and individuals anywhere in the United States. In-person sessions are available in Shawnee, OK.

Do you offer sessions in Spanish?

Yes. Coaching is available in English and Spanish, and so is this entire site.

What happens in the first conversation?

A straightforward conversation about where things stand and what the next honest step is, with no pressure and no judgment. You can book one online or call (405) 822-8300.

What is marriage restoration?

An intensive, guided path for marriages on the brink: nine sessions of proven steps that move a couple from separation back toward a lasting covenant. It is more structured than general couples coaching.
