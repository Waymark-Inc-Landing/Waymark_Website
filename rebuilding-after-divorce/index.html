Questions people ask first

Is this grief counseling?

No. It is coaching: practical, structured work on moving forward. We are not licensed counselors or therapists, and if licensed care is what you need we will say so and help you find it.

Will you tell me I should have stayed?

No. We start from where you are today, with no judgment about how you got here.

Is it too late if the divorce was years ago?

No. Many people come long after the divorce, when they notice the same wounds showing up again.
