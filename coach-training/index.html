Before you begin

Questions people ask first

What is the Talley method?

The structured approach to reconciliation that Dr. Jim Talley developed and taught over more than fifty years of work with couples. Waymark Inc. carries it forward.

Do I need to be a licensed counselor?

No. This is coach training. It does not make you a licensed counselor or therapist, and it teaches you when to refer a couple to licensed care.

Is the training available in Spanish?

Yes. We train coaches and leaders in English and Spanish.
