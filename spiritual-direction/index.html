Questions people ask first

What is spiritual direction?

Regular one-on-one conversations with someone who helps you pay attention to what God is doing in your life and respond to it honestly. It is guidance, not therapy.

Do I need to belong to a particular church?

No. Our work is Christian and rooted in Scripture, and you are welcome wherever you are in your faith.

Is it only for individuals?

Spiritual direction is usually one-on-one. If the question is about your marriage, couples coaching may be the better starting place, and we will tell you so.

Good to know

There is help paying for coaching
